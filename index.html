ABOUT ME

Good facility management starts with good planning.

A facility is more than a building. It is an environment that must support people, business activities, and everyday operations.

My approach to facility management centres on coordinating maintenance, monitoring essential services, identifying operational challenges, and supporting a safe and comfortable environment.

I believe that effective facility management requires proactive planning, clear communication, responsible resource use, and consistent follow-through.

I am also interested in how technology and artificial intelligence can support predictive maintenance, energy efficiency, cost control, and better operational decisions.
